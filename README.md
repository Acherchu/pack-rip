# Pack Rip

Open any Pokémon TCG booster pack ever sold, with real pull rates, and keep what you pull in an
online collection your friends can see.

**Play:** https://acherchu.github.io/pack-rip/

- Every set from Base Set to today, shown as its real pack
- Real pull rates for each set (and god packs where the real sets have them)
- Card prices from TCGplayer's market price
- PokéBux: packs cost their real price; your best Pokémon earn more over time
- Pokémon and number wheels

Card data and pictures come from the Pokémon TCG API, TCGdex and PokéAPI; prices and pack photos
from TCGplayer (via tcgcsv.com). A fan project — not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company,
Creatures or Game Freak. Pokémon and its names are trademarks of their respective owners.
